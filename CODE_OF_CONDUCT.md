# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be kind. Be useful. Assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## What we expect

- Empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect for different opinions and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Owning mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the people using this work.

## What is not okay

- Sexualized language or imagery, or sexual attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or email, without permission.
- Spam, including unsolicited promotion in issues and pull requests.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Shashwat Jain maintains this project and enforces this code. He may remove, edit or
reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break it, and may
ban anyone from the project for behavior he considers harmful.

To report a problem, message Shashwat privately on
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/shashbuilds), or use GitHub's
"Report content" option on the comment or issue. Reports are handled privately.

Consequences scale with the problem: a private note, then a public warning, then a
temporary ban, then a permanent ban.

## Scope

This code applies in this repository and anywhere someone represents the project in
public.
