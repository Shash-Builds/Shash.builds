# Power: Sales

For finding and pitching small businesses: leads, site audits, sample sites, cold emails, follow-ups.

## Builds on
**lead-finder**, **site-audit**, **mock-builder**, **cold-email-drafter** (each in `skills/<name>/skills/<name>/`),
the **writer** power for every word sent.

## Method
1. Confirm the trade, city, how many leads, and the offer (price and what is included).
2. Find leads from public sources only; audit each site; rank weakest first.
3. For the top few: 3 provable problems in plain words, a sample page marked "sample", a short email
   sequence (first email under 90 words, follow-ups on day 3 and day 7).
4. Log every prepared message with its follow-up date (for example `outreach/log.csv`).

## Rules
The user sends; you prepare. Respect anti-spam law (real sender, address, opt-out). No fake reviews, no
claims you can't prove, no scraping behind logins.

## Done when
A ranked list, an audit per lead, drafts in one copy block each, a log with follow-up dates.
