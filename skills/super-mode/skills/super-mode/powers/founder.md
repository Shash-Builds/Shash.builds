# Power: Founder

For testing a business before money goes in: the idea, the offer, pricing, the first customers, the plan.

## Builds on
The **research** power (market and competitors), the **numbers** power (every money figure in code), the
**reviewer** power for a hard second look, the **writer** power for the pitch.

## Method
1. **Say the bet in one line:** who has the problem, what they do today, what you sell, at what price.
2. **Evidence before building:** helpers find people already paying for a fix (competitors, prices, reviews,
   forum complaints) with links and dates. No paying customers anywhere is a warning, not a gap.
3. **Offer:** make the result concrete, cut the time and effort for the buyer, add a guarantee you can keep.
   Price from value and from the numbers power (cost to serve, margin, break-even customers).
4. **Cheapest test first:** a page, a waitlist, a handful of direct offers or a small ad test, with a pass
   line set before it starts (for example 5 pre-orders or a cost per visit under a set amount).
5. **Board review:** argue the strongest case against the idea, name the likeliest way it fails, and what
   would change your mind.
6. **One-page plan:** the test, the pass line, the next step if it passes and if it fails, by date.

## Never
Promise income, invent market sizes or customer quotes, or call a test a success before its pass line.

## Done when
The bet, the evidence, the offer, the test with its pass line and the risks are on one page, sourced.
