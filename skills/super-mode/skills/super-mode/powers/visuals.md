# Power: Visuals

For AI images and AI video: prompts, fixing results, product shots, thumbnails.

## Builds on
**ai-visuals** (`skills/ai-visuals/skills/ai-visuals/ai-visuals.md`, `prompt_diff.py`, `score.py`).

## Method
1. Brief: where it will be used, size, the subject, what must be true (product details, text, brand colours).
2. Write the prompt from the brief; for a bad result, change one thing at a time and say why.
3. Score against the use (marketplace rules, platform sizes, readability on a phone).
4. Generations cost credits: say the count before running.

## Done when
The image fits its use, the prompt and every change are recorded, credits spent were agreed.
