# Power: Clipping

For paid clipping campaigns and Shorts from long videos.

## Builds on
**clipper** (`skills/clipper/skills/clipper/clipper.md`: `clip.py find|render`), **transcriber**, the
**video-studio** power for the edit and sound.

## Method
1. Read the campaign brief and restate its rules in a short checklist: allowed footage only, required
   overlay or logo, caption text and hashtags, no links, audience rules (for example a share from the US,
   UK, Canada and Australia), engagement floor, how long clips stay up, warm-up steps, how to submit, pay
   per 1,000 views and the cap. Get the user's yes on the checklist.
2. Find moments that stand alone (a reaction, a fail, a reveal), hook in the first 1 to 2 seconds.
3. Cut 9:16 with word-by-word captions clear of the buttons, the overlay for the whole clip, clean sound.
4. Each account gets its own clips and captions; never post the same clip on several accounts.
5. Track every clip (account, link, views, payout) in a sheet.

## Rules
Only footage the campaign allows; no other creators' edits; no fake engagement or bought views.
The user posts and submits.

## Done when
Every clip passes the brief's checklist, captions and overlay are right, the tracker is updated.
