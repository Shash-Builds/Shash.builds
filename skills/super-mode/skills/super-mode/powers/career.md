# Power: Career

For job hunting: finding roles, CVs, cover emails, follow-ups, offers.

## Builds on
**ai-resume** (`skills/ai-resume/skills/ai-resume/SKILL.md`), the **writer** power, the **research** method.

## Method
1. Read the user's targets from memory: role types, experience band, cities, pay floor, no-gos, and the
   channel they want (for example email only).
2. Find fresh roles with helpers (founder posts and job boards from the last 7 to 14 days). Open every
   listing: still open, real company, posted date, pay, experience asked, the contact shown on the page.
   Drop scams (any fee to apply), internships and roles outside the targets. Never guess an email.
3. Shortlist about 10; the user picks. Write each email (under 120 words): one line on their business, one
   proof that fits their world, one clear offer. Attach the CV the user approved.
4. Log every send with a follow-up date (5 days). One follow-up with something new, never "just checking in".
5. Offers: compare fixed vs variable pay, commute cost and time, and the user's floor in a small table.

## Rules
Facts only from the user's own story file. Respect their CV rules (what goes under experience vs links).
No interview prep unless asked. Send times: weekday mornings.

## Done when
Every role verified, emails in one copy block each, the log updated, follow-ups dated.
