About

We learned on our own red clay.

Athens clay is hard in August and soup in March. Most plants from a big-box store don't make it through their first summer here.

We plant what works: natives and tough perennials that handle the heat, feed the pollinators and need less water once they're settled.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

When is the best time to plant?

Fall. Roots grow through the mild winter, so plants are ready before the summer heat. Spring works too with more watering.

Can you work with our clay soil?

Yes. We amend beds with compost and pick plants that like clay, so you're not fighting it every year.

Do you offer weekly mowing?

Yes, weekly or every other week from April to October, with edging and blowing included.

Can you fix water pooling by the house?

Usually. A French drain, a dry creek bed or regrading moves the water where it should go. We'll show you options on the yard walk.

How much does a design cost?

The yard walk and sketch are free. You get a written price for the full project before anything is planted.
