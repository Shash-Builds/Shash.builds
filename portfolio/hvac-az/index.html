FAQ

Questions people ask first

How fast can you come out?

Call before 2pm on a weekday and we aim to be there the same day. In peak summer we give you a two-hour window.

Should I repair or replace my AC?

If the system is under ten years old and the repair is less than a third of a new one, repair usually wins. We'll show you both numbers.

What does a tune-up include?

Coil cleaning, refrigerant and electrical checks, drain line flush, filter change and a written report with photos.

Do you work on heat pumps?

Yes, repairs and new installs for all major brands.

Is there a fee to come out?

There's a flat diagnostic fee, and it's waived if you go ahead with the repair.
