FAQ

Questions people ask first

Do you charge by the hour?

No. You get one flat price for the job before any work starts, so you're never watching the clock.

Can you come today?

Usually. Call before noon and most jobs in Dayton and nearby get a plumber the same day.

Should I get a tank or tankless water heater?

Tank costs less up front. Tankless costs more but saves space and runs longer. We'll price both for your house.

Why does my sump pump matter so much?

In Ohio basements it's the only thing between spring rain and a flooded floor. A battery backup keeps it running when the power goes out.

Do you clean up after?

Yes. Shoe covers, drop cloths and a full cleanup are part of every job.
