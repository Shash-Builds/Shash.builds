FAQ

Questions people ask first

Do I need to be home?

No. Most clients give us a door code or a key. Your cleaner texts when they arrive and when they leave.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes, everything, including the vacuum. If you'd like us to use your own products, leave them out and we will.

Are you OK with pets?

Yes. Tell us their names and any rooms they should stay out of.

How do I pay?

By card after each clean. You're never charged before the work is done.

What if I need to skip or move a clean?

Text us 24 hours ahead and we'll move it at no charge.
