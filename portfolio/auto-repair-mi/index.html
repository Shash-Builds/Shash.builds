FAQ

Questions people ask first

Do I need an appointment?

It helps. Booked cars get looked at first, but we take walk-ins for quick jobs like oil changes and tire swaps.

How will I know what's wrong?

We text photos and a short note for every problem, with the price to fix each one. Nothing gets done without your OK.

Can I bring my own parts?

For some jobs, yes. We can't warranty the part itself, only our labor.

Do you work on all makes?

Most domestic and import cars, SUVs and light trucks. Call if you're not sure.

How do I get home while my car is in?

We'll drive you anywhere within 5 miles of the shop and pick you up when it's ready.
