About

A local crew, not a storm chaser.

When hail hits San Antonio, trucks from three states over show up the next morning. Most are gone by the time a leak shows up in the fall.

We live here. The same project lead runs your job from the first inspection to the final walk-through, and picks up the phone if something comes up a year later.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Will my insurance cover hail damage?

It depends on your policy and the damage. We photograph every hit so your adjuster has what they need, and we can meet them on the roof.

How long does a new roof take?

Most homes take one to two days, plus time for cleanup and a final walk-through with you.

How much does a new roof cost?

It depends on size, pitch and material. The free inspection gets you a written number, so you're never guessing.

Do you install metal roofs?

Yes. Standing seam and exposed fastener panels, in colors that keep the attic cooler.

Do I need to be home?

Only for the inspection walk-through and the final check. During the build we just need the driveway clear.
