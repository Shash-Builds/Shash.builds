About

A barbershop that runs on time.

Most shops overbook, so you wait forty minutes and then get a rushed cut. We book every chair with room to breathe.

Your barber has the time to ask what you want, do it properly and finish with a hot towel. Then you're on with your day.

How it works

Booked in a minute.

  1. Pick a barber and time

    See every open chair for the week and choose the one that fits.

  2. Get a reminder

    A text the day before. Running late? Reply and we'll shuffle.

  3. Sit back

    Hot towel, a cut that takes the time it needs, and you're out the door.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, when a chair is free. Booking online is the only way to skip the wait.

How long does a cut take?

About 30 minutes for a cut, 45 for a skin fade and an hour for a cut and beard.

Do you cut curly and textured hair?

Yes. Every barber in the shop cuts all hair types.

Can I book for my kid?

Yes. Kids' cuts are for 12 and under, and you can book two chairs back to back.

Where do I park?

Metered street parking out front, free after 6pm and on Sundays.
