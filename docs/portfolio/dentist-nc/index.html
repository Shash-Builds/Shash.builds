About

Dentistry without the rush.

Plenty of people put off the dentist because the last visit felt rushed, painful or full of surprises on the bill.

We book longer appointments, explain every step before we take it, and give you the price in writing before any treatment. You can always say stop.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Do you take my insurance?

We accept most PPO plans and file the claim for you. Call or send the form with your plan name and we'll check before your visit.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Yes. Tell us when you book. We go slower, explain everything first and agree on a hand signal so you can stop anytime.

How old should my child be for a first visit?

Around their first birthday, or when the first tooth shows up. Early visits are short and mostly about getting comfortable.

Do you see emergencies?

Yes. Call first thing and we'll fit you in the same day for toothaches, chips or a lost filling or crown.

What if I don't have insurance?

You get the full price list up front, and we can spread larger treatments over a few visits.
