Turn your 365-day business goal into a game you actually log every night.

Tell Claude your goal, what you sell, where clients come from and the daily habits that move the needle. It builds your own dashboard and saves it as your private Claude artifact.

What you get:
- Levels with deadlines and boss fights, from day 1 to the top level, named after the role model you chase
- Daily quests with XP, a streak that doubles your XP after 7 days, and a mini-boss for the first weeks
- Money against pace: a chart of what you earned next to the line you need to hit your goal
- An optional 15-day plan: a short task list per day that you tick off
- A funnel from contacts to replies to calls to sales, with rates for the last 7 days and all time
- Revenue by product and channel, a content log and a Sunday weekly review

Where your data lives: in your own private Claude artifact (it follows you across devices), or in your browser on one device with Export and Import buttons. Nothing goes to anyone else.

Try it first with made-up sample data, then start your own.
