Make the daily video in one command instead of an hour in an editor.

You write a short video.md: where the voice and face clip are, and a shot list (at 0:04 show this screenshot with this line of text). The skill transcribes the voice locally, cuts every shot to the timeline, burns in word-by-word captions with the current word highlighted, adds each shot's on-screen text, lays an optional music bed under the voice and sets loudness to -14 LUFS, the level Instagram, TikTok and YouTube expect.

What you get:
- A 1080x1920, 30 fps MP4 ready to post as a reel, short or TikTok
- Shots: your face clip, a screenshot or screen recording (stills get a slow push-in), or a title card
- Captions in a clean brand style: cream words on a dark box, the spoken word in orange
- An optional closing stinger clip and a credits file for the music

Before rendering, a dry run prints every shot with its start and end, so you catch mistakes early.

Requirements: ffmpeg, Python with Pillow, and the Transcribe tool for captions (or bring your own word timings). Everything runs on your computer, nothing is uploaded.
