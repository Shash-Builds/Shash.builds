Stop resizing the same photo eight times, and stop posting your home's location by accident.

Give this skill one photo and it makes every size you post at: Instagram portrait, story and square, LinkedIn landscape and square, YouTube thumbnail, Dribbble shot and the Open Graph image a link preview uses. Each crop is centered on the subject, not just the middle of the frame, and every file is compressed.

Privacy is always on. Phone photos carry EXIF data: camera, time and often the exact GPS location where they were taken. Every output is saved with no metadata at all, and the report tells you what was removed. The photo's rotation is applied first, so nothing ends up sideways.

Optional background removal runs on your computer with rembg (U2-Net model, downloaded once). You get a transparent PNG cut-out, or the subject on a flat color of your choice.

Requirements: Python with Pillow; rembg for background removal. No uploads, no API key.
