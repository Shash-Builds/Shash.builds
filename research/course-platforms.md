## Ranking logic

Reach per hour of setup = expected reach from a platform, divided by setup hours.

Three rules set the order:
1. Marketplace discovery counts most. A platform that shows your course to people who are not yet your fans beats a storefront with no traffic. Udemy, Skillshare and YouTube search all do this.
2. Free or near-free listings rank above paid ones. Paid plans (Podia, Teachable, Thinkific, Kajabi) rank low until revenue covers the fee.
3. Closed or application-only platforms rank last. They have no reach for a solo creator unless the application passes.

Audience numbers are mostly unverified. Because of that, the ranking is an ordering, not a score. Re-rank once the audience numbers are confirmed on official pages.

Top four, short reasons:
- YouTube: free, long-lived search traffic, works for free and paid lessons.
- Udemy: marketplace browse traffic. Free course is a cheap test. The 37% organic cut is the main cost.
- Product Hunt: free one-day launch. Good for a free tool or website template pack.
- Hugging Face: free demo Spaces and learning content for AI and Claude builders.

## Per-platform detail

### 1. YouTube
- Monthly cost: $0 to publish.
- Cut: Ad revenue share is 55% to the creator on long-form video and 45% on Shorts pool, per third-party sources. The Shopify page that reversed the split is wrong. Memberships: 70% to creator (third-party). Not checked against YouTube Help Center.
- Audience: unverified.
- Buyer emails: not checked. YouTube does not give a buyer list.
- India payout: unverified. AdSense payouts were not checked for India.
- Setup hours (estimate): 8 to 12 for a channel, one free course series, and a paid course link.
- Verdict: Best free reach for tutorials. Pair with a paid course on another platform.

### 2. Udemy
- Monthly cost: $0 to list.
- Cut: 37% of sales with no instructor coupon or link. 97% of sales through the instructor's own coupon or referral link (official Udemy support page, quoted in search results). Udemy can discount prices in promotions, which can lower instructor earnings (third-party summary of terms).
- Audience: about 80 to 84 million learners, aggregator estimate (grabon, skillademia, and similar pages, 2025 to 2026). Udemy's investor site did not load. Treat as unverified official.
- Buyer emails: unverified.
- India payout: unverified.
- Free course rules: free courses get no certificate. Q&A and direct messages are not included, even if enabled (official help article, search result). A 2-hour length cap for free courses appears in older Udemy text. Current status is unverified. Free courses cannot be switched to paid without premium instructor registration.
- Setup hours (estimate): free course 4 to 6. Paid course 25 to 40.
- Verdict: Real marketplace discovery. Good first channel. Organic cut is low, so use coupons and links where possible.

### 3. Product Hunt
- Monthly cost: $0 for launch (listing rules not fetched).
- Cut: none. Not a seller.
- Audience: Semrush shows 3.07M to 5.43M monthly visits in its snippet, year not clear (third-party). Another estimate says about 4.16M monthly visits (creati.ai). A third estimate says 68.7K monthly organic search visits (instantdomainsearch). Estimates disagree. Unverified.
- Buyer emails: no.
- India payout: not applicable.
- Setup hours (estimate): 3 to 5 for a launch page, screenshots, and a short video.
- Verdict: One-day traffic spike. Use it for a free tool, a template pack, or a demo. Weak for paid courses.

### 4. Hugging Face (Spaces and Learn)
- Monthly cost: free basic Spaces (docs say free, but forum reports say free CPU Basic may be restricted from July 2026, unverified). PRO $9 per month (third-party). Team and Enterprise prices conflict between sources.
- Cut: none built in. Paid courses are not a native feature (unverified).
- Learn: a 2026 third-party guide says 12 open courses on Hugging Face Learn, free account for exercises.
- Audience: unverified.
- Buyer emails: no.
- India payout: not applicable.
- Setup hours (estimate): 4 to 6 for a demo Space and a course page.
- Verdict: Good for free AI and Claude-related demos. Not a sales channel on its own. Check current free-tier rules before you build on it.

### 5. Ko-fi
- Monthly cost: $0 (free plan). Gold $12 per month, 0% service fee (Ko-fi help center, search result). Some guides still say $6, which one source calls out of date.
- Cut: free plan takes 5% on shop sales. Gold removes that fee. Stripe or PayPal fee on top, typically 2.9% + $0.30 (third-party).
- Audience: unverified.
- Buyer emails: unverified.
- India payout: unverified.
- Setup hours (estimate): 2 to 3.
- Verdict: Cheap storefront for a PDF or a template pack. No built-in traffic.

### 6. Gumroad
- Monthly cost: $0. No monthly fee (fetched official pricing page).
- Cut: 10% + $0.50 on direct sales. 30% on sales that come from Discover to new customers (fetched official page). Processor fee not separately listed on the page.
- Audience: Discover traffic unverified.
- Buyer emails: unverified. The Gumroad help page did not give the answer.
- India payout: conflicting. One blog says India is on the direct bank payout list with INR. Another says PayPal Connect excludes India, and a Wise blog says Stripe cannot be connected in India. Payoneer and Wise are not supported (official help article, search result). Unverified.
- Setup hours (estimate): 3 to 4.
- Verdict: Simple storefront. Discover is a discovery option with a high cut. Confirm the India payout route before listing.

### 7. Payhip
- Monthly cost: Free Forever $0. Plus $29 per month. Pro $99 per month (fetched official pricing page).
- Cut: Free 5%. Plus 2%. Pro 0%. PayPal or Stripe fee on top (fetched).
- Audience: unverified.
- Buyer emails: unverified.
- India payout: not confirmed. Payhip help center has pages for Razorpay and PayU connections (search result), which suggests an India route. Stripe article lists over 40 countries (help page text cut off before India). Unverified.
- Setup hours (estimate): 3 to 4.
- Verdict: Good free start for digital downloads. Confirm India route first.

### 8. Lemon Squeezy
- Monthly cost: $0 (ecommerce plan, fetched).
- Cut: 5% + $0.50 per transaction (fetched). Some fees may apply for extra payment types (page note).
- Merchant of record: Lemon Squeezy is the seller of record and handles tax (third-party description).
- Audience: unverified.
- Buyer emails: unverified.
- India payout: unclear. Sources conflict. One says PayPal is needed. Another says India appears on the bank payout list with a note. Official supported-country text was not found.
- Payout timing: twice monthly (third-party), $50 minimum (third-party). Fetched page says more than 200 countries supported.
- Setup hours (estimate): 4 to 5.
- Verdict: Good for selling a course with tax handled by the platform. Confirm India payouts before choosing it.

### 9. itch.io
- Monthly cost: $0.
- Cut: 10% default revenue share. Processor $0.30 + 2.9% subtracted from the price (official docs, search result). Some users report 30% set manually.
- Payouts: staff review. Minimum 7 days after creating a payout. Typical 10 to 14 days.
- Audience: unverified.
- Buyer emails: unverified.
- India payout: unverified. Forum posts mention PayPal and Payoneer. UPI is not supported for buyers in India (forum).
- Setup hours (estimate): 2 to 3.
- Verdict: Built for games and assets. Weak fit for a course. Fits downloadable website templates or Claude skill packs as assets.

### 10. Stan Store
- Monthly cost: no permanent free plan. 14-day trial. Creator $29 per month or $300 per year. Creator Pro $99 per month or $948 per year (third-party, multiple sources).
- Cut: 0% platform fee per most sources. One source lists 5% and is not credible. Processor about 2.9% + $0.30 (third-party).
- Audience: unverified.
- Buyer emails: unverified.
- India payout: unverified.
- Setup hours (estimate): 2 to 3.
- Verdict: Clean paid storefront with a 0% platform cut. No free tier.

### 11. Substack
- Monthly cost: $0.
- Cut: 10% of paid subscriptions (third-party). Stripe about 2.9% + $0.30 on top. Substack fees page did not load (404). Combined estimates 13 to 16 percent (third-party).
- Audience: unverified.
- Buyer emails: writer owns the list (not checked on an official page).
- India payout: unverified.
- Setup hours (estimate): 3 to 4.
- Verdict: Owned list. Good for a newsletter that sells a course. No marketplace traffic.

### 12. Skillshare
- Monthly cost: $0 to teach.
- Cut: teachers share a royalty pool. Pool is 30% of membership revenue (third-party guide). Rate changes monthly. No fixed per-minute rate. Typical $0.05 to $0.10 per minute is a third-party estimate, not confirmed.
- Eligibility, from Skillshare help center notices (search results):
  - From 2026-01-01: 50 followers and 75 minutes monthly minimum.
  - Later update (search result, effective date not seen): 400 minutes watched with a class published in the last 18 months, or 650 minutes without one.
- Audience: about 13 million registered users and about 500,000 paying subscribers, 2024 (secondary source).
- Buyer emails: no.
- India payout: unverified.
- Setup hours (estimate): 10 to 20.
- Verdict: Good reach if accepted. Income is unpredictable. Needs followers first.

### 13. Patreon
- Monthly cost: $0.
- Cut: 10% platform fee for pages published after 2025-08-04 (official support article, search result). Older pages keep older rates, but unpublishing moves them to 10%. Processing 2.9% + $0.30 on USD pages (third-party). Currency conversion about 2.5% (third-party).
- Audience: unverified.
- Buyer emails: unverified.
- India payout: unverified.
- Setup hours (estimate): 3 to 4.
- Verdict: Membership model for an ongoing community. Less suited to a one-time course.

### 14. Maven
- Monthly cost: unverified.
- Cut: 10% of paid enrollments (help center, search result). Company seats split 50/50 (secondary). Some older text mentions 25% for add-on services (unverified, undated).
- Application: required. Terms not fetched.
- Audience: unverified.
- Buyer emails: unverified.
- India payout: unverified.
- Setup hours (estimate): 15 to 25.
- Verdict: Paid cohort courses. Good for live workshops. Not a free test.

### 15. Podia
- Monthly cost: Mover $49 (annual $42). Shaker $99. Earthquaker $179. No free plan. 14-day trial (fetched).
- Cut: Mover 5%. Shaker and Earthquaker 0% from Podia. Processor 2.9% + 30c (fetched).
- Audience: unverified. Buyer emails: unverified. India payout: unverified.
- Setup hours (estimate): 6 to 10.
- Verdict: Paid only. Skip until revenue covers it.

### 16. Teachable
- Monthly cost: Starter $39. Builder $89. Growth $189. Custom by contact. No free plan. 7-day trial (fetched). A pop-up on the page advertised 30 days.
- Cut: Starter 7.5%. Builder and Growth 0% with Teachable Payments. Processor fees on top.
- Audience: unverified. Buyer emails: unverified. India payout: unverified.
- Setup hours (estimate): 6 to 10.
- Verdict: Paid only. Skip for a first course.

### 17. Thinkific
- Monthly cost: Start $109 (annual $82). Grow $219 (annual $164). Expand $499 (annual $374). Plus by sales. Add-on mobile app $199 (fetched).
- Cut: Thinkific Payments 2.9%. Other gateways add fees, not quantified (fetched). Free plan: none. 30-day trial.
- Audience: unverified. Buyer emails: unverified. India payout: unverified.
- Setup hours (estimate): 8 to 12.
- Verdict: Too costly at launch.

### 18. Kajabi
- Monthly cost: Basic $179 (annual $143 per month). Growth $249 ($199). Pro $499 ($399). No free plan. Money-back guarantee 30 days (fetched).
- Cut: not checked.
- Audience, buyer emails, India payout: unverified.
- Setup hours (estimate): 10 to 15.
- Verdict: Shows that the top end is not cheap. Skip.

### 19. LinkedIn Learning
- Not a seller. Instructors apply through an online form (official instructor page, search result). Form asks for a sample video and language. Selection looks for on-camera teaching and deep expertise (third-party quote).
- Pay terms, audience, and India payout: unverified.
- Setup hours (estimate): 6 to 10 for a sample video and the form.
- Verdict: Long shot for a solo creator.

### 20. Domestika
- Not checked on official pages. Official teacher terms did not show in search.
- Advance model, per a teacher blog (third-party). A 50% share claim is unreliable (unverified).
- Teachers are invited. Production is done in-house per 2022 funding news (search result).
- Setup hours: application required (unknown).
- Verdict: Curated. Apply only if the course fits its creative focus.

### 21. Coursera
- Not open to individual instructors, as far as found. Partner pathways are for universities and companies (search results, not official text).
- Audience: 205 million cumulative registered learners at Q1 2026, per Coursera release on SEC (search result, dated 2026-03-31 filing). Udemy combination completed 2026-05-11 (secondary source).
- Verdict: Not feasible for a solo creator unless a partner pathway applies.

### 22. edX
- Partner institutions only, per forum post and third-party review (not official). Official edX partner pages were not fetched.
- Verdict: Not feasible for a solo creator.
