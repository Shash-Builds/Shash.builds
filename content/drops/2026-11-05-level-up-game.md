I built my life into a game. Here it is for you.

On 3 November I set one goal for the year.

Then I made it a game I play every night:

Levels with deadlines. Level 1 is my first sale. The last level is named after the person I'm chasing.

Boss fights. Miss a deadline and it says "overdue" in red.

Daily quests. 50 cold emails, 10 follow-ups, 1 post. Each one is worth XP.

A streak. Log 7 nights in a row and every point counts double.

And a chart of money earned against the line I need to hit.

Logging a day is built to take under a minute: tap a few counters, hit save.

Today I'm giving it away. Free, open source, MIT.

You tell Claude your goal, what you sell and the habits that get you clients. It builds your version and saves it to your own private Claude artifact. Or just open it in a browser.

The screenshot is sample data. My real numbers stay in my own copy.

Get it here: https://github.com/Shash-Builds/shash.builds/tree/HEAD/tools/level-up-dashboard
